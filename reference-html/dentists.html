Questions from dental practices

How long does dental SEO take to show results?

Google Business Profile improvements often move rankings within weeks. Website and treatment page rankings usually build over three to six months, and we report on calls, forms and bookings along the way.

Do you work with a single practice or a group?

Both. We manage single-location practices and multi-location groups, with a page and a Google profile for each location.

Can you manage ads for implants and Invisalign?

Yes. We build separate campaigns and landing pages for each treatment so budget goes to the cases you want most.

Will I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, Google profile and ad accounts stay in your name.
