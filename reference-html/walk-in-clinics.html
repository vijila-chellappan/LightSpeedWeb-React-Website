Walk-in patients choose on convenience, hours and trust.

Convenience wins

Distance, open hours and how quickly a patient will be seen decide most visits. Your listing and site have to answer all three at a glance.

Coverage questions block bookings

Patients want to know about OHIP, who is seen and what it costs before they leave home. Clear answers raise visits.

Repeat visits build the clinic

Reminders, reviews and easy rebooking turn one-time visitors into patients who return and refer others.
