Urgent searches are decided in seconds, on a phone, by whoever shows as open.

Hours must be exact

A wrong closing time sends patients to a competitor and earns a one-star review. Holiday and late hours need to be correct on Google every time.

Mobile and click-to-call come first

Most urgent care visitors are on a phone. The site needs hours, address, services and a call button visible without scrolling.

Services drive the search

People search for the problem, such as stitches, X-ray or a sprain. A page for each service lets you appear for it.

Questions from urgent care clinics

Can ads run only while we are open?

Yes. We schedule campaigns around your hours and can pause them when the clinic is closed or at capacity.

How do you keep our hours correct?

We manage your Google profile, including holiday and special hours, and update it whenever your schedule changes.

Can you show wait times on the site?

If your system provides wait time or queue data, we can display it on the site. We can also add online check-in.

Do you handle several locations?

Yes. Each location gets its own page and Google profile.
